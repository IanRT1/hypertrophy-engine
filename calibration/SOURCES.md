# Calibration Sources

- Shimano et al. (2006), trained and untrained men performing squat, bench press, and arm
  curl to failure at 60%, 80%, and 90% 1RM: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17194239/
- Halperin et al. (2022), test-retest strength-endurance profiles at 70%, 80%, and 90% bench
  press 1RM: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35511896/
- Nuzzo et al. (2023), meta-regression of 952 repetitions-to-failure tests from 269 studies:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37792272/
- Willardson and Burkett (2006), five bench-press sets at 50% and 80% 1RM with 1-, 2-, and
  3-minute rests: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16686570/
- Senna et al. (2013), greater upper-body session volume with 3-minute versus 1-minute rest:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24150002/
- Pareja-Blanco et al. (2017), recovery following failure and non-failure resistance training
  through 72 hours: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28965198/
- Thomas et al. (2018), neuromuscular fatigue persisting 48–72 hours after repeated maximal
  efforts: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30067591/

These studies support directional and broad-range checks. They do not validate a universal
individual prescription or the app's long-term hypertrophy coefficients.
