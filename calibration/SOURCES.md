# Calibration Sources

- **STRENGTH-001:** Mayhew et al. (1992), cross-validation of repetitions-to-failure
  equations for bench press; Brzycki was most accurate at 10 or fewer repetitions:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7500624/
- **STRENGTH-002:** Roberts et al. (2025), exercise-specific cross-validation recommends
  using loads permitting 4-10 repetitions:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39495260/
- **REPS-001:** Shimano et al. (2006), exercise-specific repetitions to failure:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17194239/
- **REPS-002:** Halperin et al. (2022), strength-endurance test-retest profiles:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35511896/
- **REST-001:** Willardson and Burkett (2006), inter-set rest and bench-press volume:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16686570/
- **REST-002:** Senna et al. (2013), rest duration and upper-body session volume:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24150002/
- **RECOVERY-001:** Pareja-Blanco et al. (2017), recovery through 72 hours:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28965198/
- **RECOVERY-002:** Thomas et al. (2018), neuromuscular fatigue after maximal efforts:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30067591/

## Additional synthesis

- Nuzzo et al. (2023), meta-regression of 952 repetitions-to-failure tests from 269 studies:
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37792272/

These studies support directional and broad-range checks. They do not validate a universal
individual prescription or the app's long-term hypertrophy coefficients.
